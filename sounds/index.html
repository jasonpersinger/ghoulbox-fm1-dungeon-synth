GHOULBOX Sounds

All 59 sounds of GHOULBOX, the dungeon-synth firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, across 13 engines. Each plays the pattern its preset suggests, through the hall reverb and tape the FM-1 starts with, rendered by the firmware's own sound code. Sounds marked GHOULBOX are new; the rest come from Felucca.
