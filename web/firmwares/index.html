FM-1 Firmwares

A directory of free, open-source replacement firmwares for the M-VAVE FM-1 synthesizer. Discover community creations, browse source code, test instruments directly in your browser, and listen to sound previews.

Listings sourced from the FM-1 Firmware Hub by Sven Trogus. To install, switch, or backup firmwares on your FM-1, connect your device via USB in Chrome or Edge and use the Firmware Hub.
