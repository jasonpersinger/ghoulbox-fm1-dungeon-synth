Welcome to the dungeon

GHOULBOX turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a dungeon-synth instrument: hurdy-gurdies and drones, crypt pads and monk choirs, organs, harps and recorders, all through a cathedral reverb and a worn cassette.

It is a fork of Felucca, the open-source FM-1 firmware by Hügelton Instruments. Everything Felucca does still works here: four tracks, a 64-step sequencer, songs, the arpeggiator, chord keys, effects, the web editor and backups. GHOULBOX adds the sounds, the reverb and tape, a hurdy-gurdy engine and an old-RPG look, and leaves out the sounds that don't fit the genre.

GHOULBOX is a hobby project, tested on one FM-1. Install it at your own risk. The installer backs up your FM-1 first, and you can always go back to Felucca or M-VAVE's own firmware.

1 · Install, back up, go back

What you need

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge (other browsers can't talk MIDI to the FM-1)
  • A USB data cable (some cables only charge)
  • No other music software open (it can hold on to the FM-1's MIDI port)

Install

  1. Open jasonpersinger.github.io/ghoulbox-fm1-dungeon-synth (it opens the installer at its https address).
  2. Connect the FM-1 and press Install. Allow MIDI access when the browser asks.
  3. The installer saves a backup of your music, sounds and settings first, then switches the FM-1 to update mode and writes GHOULBOX.
  4. When it says done, the FM-1 restarts with the GHOULBOX skull. Hold HOME › SYSTEM › ABOUT shows the version.
If the installer stops after it says "update mode", unplug the USB cable, plug it back in, and press Install again. The FM-1 stays in update mode until the install is finished, so nothing is lost.

Going back

To return to Felucca, install it from hugelton.github.io/Felucca and restore your backup in its editor. Its Return to official V15 button puts back M-VAVE's own firmware.

Your old music

Projects and user presets from Felucca load in GHOULBOX. Sounds GHOULBOX no longer lists (for example the techno presets or the drum kit) still load and play inside an old project; they are only missing from the menus.

2 · Five-minute start

GHOULBOX powers on ready to play a small dungeon piece.

  1. Press PLAY. Four tracks play a scene in D minor at 72 BPM: a hurdy-gurdy melody (track 1), a crypt pad (2), chanting monks (3) and a drone (4), through the hall reverb. Press PLAY again to stop.
  2. Turn MASTER for volume. Headphones in the jack silence the speaker.
  3. Turn ALGORITHM to choose the track (T1–T4). Everything you do applies to that track.
  4. Turn PRESETS to change the track's sound while it plays. Your pattern stays.
  5. Play the keys to play the selected track. OCT− / OCT+ shift the octave.
  6. Turn SELECT to change the tempo.
  7. Turn KNOB 1–4 to shape the sound: on HOME they are the engine's four main controls.
Made a mess? Hold SAVE to undo the last sound or pattern load.

3 · The panel

The FM-1's controls, as Felucca (and GHOULBOX) use them. Tap a page button for its page, tap again for the next page in its group, and hold some of them for quick layers.

Panel diagram from Felucca's documentation (Hügelton Instruments, GPL-3.0). The screen in it shows Felucca's look; GHOULBOX's is on the next page.

Quick layers

Hold FX, GLO, SCL or EDIT to put shortcuts on the keys and knobs; the screen shows the map. Double-tap one to keep its layer open; tap again to close it. In the EDIT layer, each white key picks an engine. OCT+ confirms and OCT− goes back, on every action page and dialog.

4 · The screen

GHOULBOX draws Felucca's pages as an old role-playing game would: stone windows, a pixel font, flickering wall torches beside the scope, and a sword that points at what is selected.

  • Colours: a new FM-1 starts in CRYPT (stone, bone, torch amber, blood red). If you came from Felucca, your saved palette stays: hold HOME and pick COLOR › CRYPT.
  • The header shows play state, tempo, the track number and battery.
  • Knob cards show the four values the knobs change; the one you just turned is drawn in the accent colour.
  • Messages (for example SOUND INIT or NO SAMPLE) appear in the header for a moment.

6 · The sounds

59 sounds across 13 engines. Hear them all at jasonpersinger.github.io/ghoulbox-fm1-dungeon-synth/sounds. GB marks GHOULBOX's own; the rest are Felucca's that fit the genre.

Real recordings

The SAMPLE engine plays four real instruments, recorded by Versilian Studios and released into the public domain (CC0): a bowed psaltery (a medieval zither played with a bow), a Renaissance chamber organ, a baroque tenor recorder and a folk harp. Held notes on the first three sustain for as long as you hold the key. SAMPLE's SET knob lists them first, then your own samples (USR1–USR3, uploaded from the web editor).

The hurdy-gurdy (GURDY)

A hurdy-gurdy plays one melody note at a time over drones that never stop. Its eight controls (EDIT):

ControlWhat it does
DRNThe drone's note (C–B): set it to your song's key
DLVLDrone level
5THA second drone a fifth above, and its level
WHLWheel pressure: darker when low, brighter and rougher when high
BUZZThe trompette, a buzzing bridge: how hard it rattles
COUPWhen it buzzes: always (HOLD) or rhythmic strokes in time (1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16)
BODYThe wooden body's resonance
WOBLThe wheel's unevenness: a slow wander in pitch and loudness

Drums (DRUM)

The DRUM engine's CRYPT kit is built for dungeon synth: a low frame drum, a rope snare, a hand slap, a shaker, jingles, a timpani, a wooden stick and a gong. Two sounds use it: CRYPT KIT and TOMB DRUMS (lower and drier), both suggesting the TOMBBEAT pattern. The keys follow the General MIDI drum map (C the frame drum, D the rope snare, A the timpani), and on a DRUM track SEQ › STEP is a drum grid. KIT also offers Felucca's kits (STD, 80, 10, 66, 55, 77) if you want something less ancient.

7 · Effects: hall and tape

Each track sends to shared effects; their amounts are on the FX page (KNOB 1–4: distortion, chorus, delay, reverb). Tap FX again to reach the effects themselves.

REVERB

  • TYPE (KNOB 1): ROOM, SPRING or HALL. GHOULBOX starts in HALL.
  • SIZE: from a 1.5-second stone chamber to a 12-second cathedral.
  • DAMP: darkens the tail.

TAPE

  • TAPE: the whole mix through a worn cassette: wow and flutter (wavering pitch), saturation, treble loss and hiss. GHOULBOX starts at 40.
  • CRSH: a cheap sampler: fewer bits and a lower sample rate. Off by default; a little adds grit, a lot turns to crunch.

DLY and CHORUS

A delay (time, feedback, colour, mix) and a chorus (rate, depth). With HALL selected, the delay is limited to 0.74 s, because the hall borrows part of its memory.

The FX layer

Hold FX to play performance effects from the keys (repeat, reverse, filter sweeps, tape stop, freeze, a harmonizer) and mute tracks with the black keys. With MENU › FX LATCH on, they toggle instead of needing to be held.

8 · Sequencer and patterns

Each track has its own sequence of up to 64 steps, with chords, ties, accents, slides, per-step chance and ratchets.

Load a factory pattern

SEQ › PHRASES lists ready-made patterns to load into the selected track. GHOULBOX adds nine, in D minor, over the power-on scene's chords: DIRGE (slow pad chords), BALLAD (a melody), CHANT (low voices) and PEDAL (one held low D), which the power-on scene uses, and MARCH (a stepping bass), LAMENT (a falling melody), ARPEGGIO (broken chords for harp or lute) and ANTIPHON (a call answered an octave lower); and TOMBBEAT, a slow beat for the CRYPT drums. Loading a sound never changes your pattern, and loading a pattern never changes your sound.

Write your own

  • Step entry: on SEQ › STEP, move the cursor with PRESETS and play a key to write it into the step.
  • Live recording: press REC to arm the track (from any page), then play while the sequence runs; it loops and overdubs.
  • Length and speed: SEQ › PATTERN sets the length (LEN) and step size (DIV, from 4 bars down to 1/32). The power-on scene uses 1/8 steps for its slow pace.
  • Clear: hold REC on a SEQ page; OCT+ clears, OCT− cancels.

Scales and chords

On SCL, set QNT to WHITE to play the chosen scale on the white keys only. For dungeon synth, natural minor (MIN), Dorian (DOR), Phrygian (PHRY) and harmonic minor (HARM) are all at home. Tap SCL again for CHORD: one finger then plays a chord of the scale.

Songs

Hold SEQ for SONG, to chain patterns A–D into a longer piece.

9 · Saving your work

  • Save a sound (32 user slots): stop, tap SAVE, pick a slot with KNOB 1, press OCT+, name it with the keys, and press OCT+ again.
  • Projects (4): SAVE › PROJECT keeps the whole session: all four tracks, their sounds and sequences, songs and settings.
  • Undo: hold SAVE to go back before the last sound or pattern load (hold again to redo).
  • Backups: the web editor (on the installer site) makes a full backup to your computer and restores it.
The FM-1 always starts with the dungeon scene. Your saved projects stay in their slots; load one from SAVE › PROJECT.

10 · The menu

Hold HOME for the menu. ALGORITHM moves between its four tabs, PRESETS through the rows, and any knob or OCT− / OCT+ changes a value. Press HOME to close it.

Recording to a computer

The FM-1 appears on a computer as a USB audio input named "Felucca", carrying the master output at 44.1 kHz with no driver. Set SPEAKER EQ to FLAT, and USB LEVEL to FIXED if you want a level that doesn't follow the MASTER knob.

MIDI keyboards and controllers

A MIDI keyboard or computer (USB) plays the FM-1: MIDI channels 1–4 play tracks 1–4 (MENU › SYSTEM › ROUT CH1-4), or set ROUT to SEL for every channel to play the selected track. From 1.2, these control changes turn the FM-1's controls, on the channel's track:

CCControl
7 · 10Track level · pan
73 · 75 · 79 · 72Attack · decay · sustain · release
91 · 93 · 94Reverb · chorus · delay sends
21–24The four engine knobs (the HOME page's)
85 · 86CRSH · TAPE (the whole mix)
74The FX layer's filter on the whole mix: below 64 low-pass, above high-pass, 64 off (it resets when the FX layer is let go)

On the selected track, while it is armed (REC), track moves are recorded as automation, as when turning its knob.

11 · Dungeon recipes

Starting points. Load the sound with PRESETS, then adjust.

Old Tower-style pad

CRYPT PAD or DARK STRINGS on a track, the DIRGE pattern from SEQ › PHRASES, REVERB SIZE high, TAPE around 60. Slow everything down: tempo 60–70, DIV 1/4 or 1/2.

Lone hurdy-gurdy

HURDY GURDY with DRN set to your key. Use COUP 1/8 for rhythmic buzz strokes on a dance tune, or BUZZ at zero for a sad, plain drone.

Monastery

MONKS on one track and CRYPT CHOIR an octave above on another, the CHANT pattern, REVERB HALL with SIZE at maximum. Add PIPE ORGAN or REN ORGAN holding the root.

Tavern ballad

FOLK HARP or LUTE on an arpeggio (ARP), TENOR RECORD or LOFI FLUTE for the tune, BAGPIPE for a reedy lead over its drones.

Lo-fi cassette demo

Any of the above with TAPE 80–100 and CRSH 30–50: the sound of a 1990s dungeon-synth tape.

12 · Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to try
No soundTurn up MASTER; check the track isn't muted (GLO › MIXER); headphones in the jack silence the speaker.
The installer can't find the FM-1Use a USB data cable, close other music software, use Chrome or Edge.
The installer stops after "update mode"Unplug and replug the USB cable and press Install again. The FM-1 waits in update mode until finished.
"NO SAMPLE" and a plain toneThe SAMPLE or GRAIN source is an empty user slot (USR1–3). Pick a built-in set or upload a sample in the web editor.
A keyboard or DAW plays the wrong trackMENU › SYSTEM › ROUT: CH1-4 sends channels 1–4 to tracks 1–4 and ignores 5–16; SEL sends every channel to the selected track.
No USB audio on a MacMENU › SYSTEM › USB SERIAL OFF (macOS 13–15).
The screen is black after an interrupted updateIf the computer sees a device named WL80UBOOT, the FM-1 is in its boot mode and can be recovered: try another cable and run the installer again. Felucca's README describes further recovery.
Something elseReport it at github.com/jasonpersinger/ghoulbox-fm1-dungeon-synth/issues with your OS, browser and the installer's log text.
