GHOULBOX Sounds

All {{TOTAL}} sounds of GHOULBOX, the dungeon-synth firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, across {{ENGINES}} engines. Each plays a short, slow phrase in D minor, through the hall reverb and tape the FM-1 starts with, rendered by the firmware's own sound code and levelled to one loudness. Sounds marked GHOULBOX are new; the rest come from Felucca.
